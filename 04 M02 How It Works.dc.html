HOW IT WORKS

Five loops, one calm day

Clearsky runs the same short cycle every day: see what needs you, answer it well, turn the real ones into plans, keep what you promised, and protect the time it takes.
01TRIAGE

See only what needs you

Every morning Clearsky reads across your messages, email and calendar and hands you one short stack — sorted by who is waiting and how long.
Inner circle always rises to the top Stale threads sweep away in one pass Ends on a clear sky, not an empty inbox
02RESPOND

Answer like you mean it

A familiar thread view with one difference: the actions that matter sit right above the keyboard, so a reply can become a plan without leaving the conversation.
Draft assist in your own tone Snooze without losing the thread Too big for text? Turn it into a call
03PROMISE

Keep what you said

Anything you commit to becomes a promise with a date, linked back to the message where you made it. Overdue is amber, never red.
Created automatically from your replies Kept promises log the moment Release one honestly instead of ghosting
04PROTECT

Hold the time it takes

Plans need room. Clearsky finds the open evenings before they fill and blocks them as protected — recover, deep work, or together.
Syncs to your calendar as busy Warns before a plan eats a block Open-space finder for the weekend
05REVIEW

See that you showed up

Sunday evening, a short recap: promises kept, clear-sky days, evenings protected — and one question about who you want to show up for next week.
A week in one calm screen Log a moment on any kept promise Set next week's intention in a tap
