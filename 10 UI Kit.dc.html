02

Construction & clear space

One unit, x, is the diameter of the tallest puff. Every measurement in the identity is a multiple of it.
clear space = 1x Clearsky
Clear space. No type, rule, edge or image may enter 1x on any side. In dense UI the minimum falls to 0.5x, never lower.
puff A r = 1.25xpuff B r = 1.00xpuff C r = 0.85xbaseline offset = 0.25x
Geometry. Three overlapping circles on a 20×14 field. The tall puff sits left of centre; the sun always rises from the right. Never redraw by eye.
Clearsky✗ baseline aligned Clearsky✓ optically centred
Alignment. The mark centres on the wordmark’s x-height, not its baseline. Gap between mark and wordmark is 0.3x.

04

Misuse

Eight failures we have already seen. If a proposed use resembles any of these, it is wrong.
✕ Clearsky
Never skew, rotate, arch or distort the lockup.
✕ Clearsky
Never set the wordmark in Instrument Sans or any other face.
✕ Clearsky
Never recolour the puffs or split the lockup into two colours.
✕ Clearsky
No gradients, bevels, glows or drop shadows on the wordmark.
✕ Clearsky
Never tighten the mark-to-wordmark gap below 0.3x.
✕ Clearsky
Never mirror the lockup or move the mark to the right.
✕ Clearsky
Never use a mid-ramp tint that drops the logo below 3:1 on its ground.
✕ ClearskyAI
Never append a product word, tagline or badge inside the lockup.

Minimum sizes
App body never below 12.5px · marketing body never below 15px · slide text never below 24px · print never below 12pt.
Dynamic Type
Every app token scales with the system setting. Layouts must survive 200% without truncation or overlap.
Measure
Long-form copy caps at 68 characters. App body caps at 42. Never justify; never hyphenate.
Numerals
Tabular figures for times, counts and prices. Proportional everywhere else.

Hit targets
44×44px minimum for every tappable element. Where the visual control is smaller, wrap it in a transparent 44px target and keep the visual centred.
Focus
2px ring in --focus at 2px offset, on every interactive element, never removed. Focus must be visible on both light and navy grounds.
Iconography
24×24 grid, 1.6px stroke, round caps and joins, 2px inner padding. Clouds are the only filled shapes.
Naming
Tokens are kebab-case and semantic: --ink-2, --action-quiet. Components are PascalCase. States use the product vocabulary, never synonyms.
